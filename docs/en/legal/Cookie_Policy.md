## 1. INTRODUCTION: THE "INTERFACE" MODEL

This Privacy Policy explains how the `safwa.network` website (the "Interface") handles information.

**CRITICAL DISTINCTION:**
The Interface is **NOT** a traditional web application. It does not have a backend database, user accounts, or a login server. It is strictly a **Client-Side Graphical User Interface (GUI)** that allows your web browser to communicate directly with the Safwa Smart Contract on the Polygon Blockchain.

Because of this decentralized architecture, **we do not (and cannot) collect, store, or process your personal data in the traditional sense.**

---

## 2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

To adhere to the principles of decentralization and anonymity, we practice strict **Data Minimization**. We explicitly confirm that we **DO NOT** collect, store, or have access to:

1. **Personal Identity Information (PII):** We do not collect names, physical addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, or government IDs.
2. **Account Credentials:** We do not have accounts. We do not possess passwords, seed phrases, private keys, or "Secret Recovery Phrases."
3. **Financial Data:** We do not store your bank account details or credit card numbers. All value transfer occurs peer-to-peer on the blockchain.
4. **Analytics:** We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, or third-party tracking cookies to profile your behavior.

---

## 3. THE PUBLIC BLOCKCHAIN (YOUR DATA IS PUBLIC)

While the Interface does not collect data, you must understand that **the Polygon Blockchain is a public, immutable ledger.**

### 3.1. Public Transparency

When you use the Interface to sign a transaction (Buy, Sell, Transfer), you are broadcasting data to thousands of validator nodes globally.

- **Visible Data:** Your Wallet Address (e.g., `0x123...`), the transaction amount, the time of execution, and the smart contract interactions are permanently visible to the entire world.
- **No Privacy on Chain:** Anyone can view your entire transaction history on block explorers like **PolygonScan**.

### 3.2. Immutability (No "Right to be Forgotten")

Under laws like GDPR (Europe), users typically have a "Right to Erasure" (Right to be Forgotten).

- **Technological Impossibility:** It is **impossible** to delete or modify data once it is written to the Blockchain.
- **Acknowledgment:** By using the Safwa Protocol, you acknowledge that your transaction history is permanent and cannot be deleted by us, the developers, or any government authority.

---

## 4. DATA PROCESSED BY THE INTERFACE (TECHNICAL NECESSITY)

Although we do not "collect" data, interacting with the Interface involves technical data transmission required for the software to function.

### 4.1. Local Storage (Browser Memory)

The Interface uses your browser's **Local Storage** to improve user experience. This data remains on your device and is never sent to our servers.

- **Wallet State:** We store a flag (e.g., `isConnected: true`) so you remain connected when you refresh the page.
- **Settings:** We store your preferred "Slippage Tolerance" and Theme (Dark/Light mode).

### 4.2. RPC Providers (Blockchain Connection)

To display your SAFWA balance and the current price, the Interface connects to a **Remote Procedure Call (RPC)** node (e.g., Infura, Alchemy, or Public Polygon RPC).

- **Data Transmission:** Your IP address and Wallet Address are sent to the RPC provider to fetch data.
- **Third-Party Control:** This data processing is governed by the privacy policy of the RPC provider configured in your wallet (e.g., MetaMask default settings), not by Safwa.

### 4.3. Hosting & Infrastructure

The Interface is hosted on decentralized or distributed content delivery networks (e.g., IPFS, Cloudflare, Vercel).

- **Metadata:** These providers may collect technical metadata (such as your IP address) for security purposes (DDoS protection) and load balancing.

## 5. THIRD-PARTY WALLETS

To use the Interface, you must use third-party wallet software (e.g., MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Ledger).

- **Independent Controllers:** These wallets are separate software products with their own Terms of Service and Privacy Policies.
- **No Liability:** We are not responsible for how your wallet provider handles your data. We strongly recommend reviewing their privacy policies.

## 6. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE (GDPR / CCPA)

### 6.1. Role of the Interface Operators

Under data protection laws, the Interface Operators act as **Software Developers**, not "Data Controllers." We do not determine the "purposes and means" of processing your data; the Blockchain protocol does.

### 6.2. Cross-Border Data Transfer

Since the Blockchain is a global network of nodes, your transaction data is replicated across servers in every jurisdiction worldwide. By using the Protocol, you consent to this global transfer of your public on-chain data.
