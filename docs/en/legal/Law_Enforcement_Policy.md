## PREAMBLE: STATEMENT OF INCAPACITY

**TO ALL LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES, REGULATORS, JUDICIARY BODIES, AND GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS:**

This document serves as the official policy of the Safwa Decentralized Network regarding requests for user data, asset freezing, transaction reversal, or network censorship.

The Safwa Protocol is a fully decentralized, autonomous, and immutable software utility deployed on the Polygon Blockchain. Following the initial deployment of the source code, the original developers (the "Deployers") explicitly and irrevocably **Renounced Ownership** and restricted administrative privileges to a state of nullity.

**THEREFORE, PLEASE BE ADVISED:**

There is no "Administrator," "CEO," "Controller," or "Custodian" of the Safwa Protocol. The Deployers possess **ZERO** technical capability to intervene in the Protocol's operation, movement of funds, or execution of logic. Any court order, subpoena, or warrant compelling the Deployers to manipulate the Protocol asks for the **technologically impossible**.

## ARTICLE I: DEFINITIONS AND TECHNICAL CONTEXT

To ensure Law Enforcement Agencies (LEAs) understand the limitations of our capacity to respond, the following definitions apply:

### 1.1. "Renounced Ownership"

This refers to the cryptographic act of transferring the administrative control of a Smart Contract to the `0x000...000` (Zero Address) or a non-recoverable address. Once performed, this action is irreversible. It means no human being on Earth possesses the private keys necessary to upgrade the code, pause the system, or blacklist users.

### 1.2. "Non-Custodial"

The Safwa Protocol does not hold, store, or manage user funds in a centralized database. Users maintain full and exclusive control over their Private Keys. The Protocol serves only as a mathematical pass-through mechanism for the Bonding Curve logic.

### 1.3. "Immutability"

The logic of the Safwa Protocol, once deployed to the Polygon Network, cannot be altered, deleted, or patched. The rules of the network are final and binding, enforceable only by the blockchain validators, not by the Deployers.

## ARTICLE II: LIMITATIONS ON CAPABILITY (THE "IMPOSSIBILITY" CLAUSE)

LEAs often request actions standard for Centralized Exchanges (CEXs) or Banks. Due to the decentralized architecture of Safwa, we are physically and cryptographically unable to comply with such requests.

### 2.1. Inability to Seize or Freeze Assets

The Deployers **DO NOT** possess the "Admin Keys" or "God Mode" required to manipulate user balances.

- **No Freeze Function:** The Smart Contract code does not contain a function to `freeze`, `blacklist`, or `sanction` specific wallet addresses. We cannot stop a suspect from interacting with the contract.
- **No Seizure Capability:** The USDC Reserves held in the Smart Contract are algorithmically locked to back the Bonding Curve. There is no `withdraw()` function accessible to the Deployers. The funds can only be moved if a user holding SAFWA tokens initiates a `sell()` transaction with their own private keys.

### 2.2. Inability to Reverse Transactions

- **Finality:** Transactions confirmed on the Polygon Blockchain are immutable. We cannot "roll back" the chain to undo a hack, a theft, or an illicit transfer.
- **No Chargebacks:** We cannot force a refund. Once value is sent to the Smart Contract, it is governed solely by the code.

### 2.3. Inability to Identify Users

- **Anonymity:** The Protocol functions entirely on public key cryptography. We do not require, collect, or store names, physical addresses, email addresses, or government IDs (KYC).
- **No User Accounts:** There is no database of "User Accounts" to suspend or delete.

## ARTICLE III: DATA NON-RETENTION POLICY (ZERO-LOGS)

The operators of the web interface (`safwa.network`) adhere to a strict **Data Minimization** and **Zero-Log** philosophy to protect user privacy and minimize security risks.

### 3.1. Information We DO NOT Possess

We certify that, to the best of our knowledge, our servers and databases **DO NOT** contain:

1. **Personally Identifiable Information (PII):** Names, addresses, phone numbers, or social security numbers.
2. **Access Credentials:** Passwords, seed phrases, or private keys.
3. **Historical Access Logs:** We do not retain long-term historical logs of IP addresses correlated with specific transaction hashes or wallet addresses.
4. **Financial Ledgers:** We do not maintain an internal ledger. The only ledger is the public blockchain.

### 3.2. Third-Party Infrastructure

The website interface is a static front-end that may be hosted on decentralized storage (IPFS) or third-party content delivery networks (CDNs) such as Cloudflare or Vercel.

- **Metadata:** If LEAs seek metadata (e.g., connection timestamps or IP addresses), they must direct their inquiries to these third-party infrastructure providers directly.
- **No Access:** The Deployers do not have root access to the internal logs of these third-party providers.

## ARTICLE V: GUIDELINES FOR SUBMITTING LEGAL REQUESTS

While we respect the authority of Law Enforcement, our response to legal process is strictly limited by our lack of control and lack of data.

### 5.1. Jurisdiction and Validity

- **Domesticating Orders:** The Safwa Protocol is domiciled in **Saint Vincent and the Grenadines**. Foreign subpoenas (e.g., from the USA, EU, or UK) must be formally domesticated via the Courts of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines or processed through a valid **Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT)**.
- **Service of Process:** Requests must be served upon our registered agent in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. Service via email, Telegram, Discord, or Twitter is **not accepted**.

### 5.2. Requirements for a Response

If an LEA chooses to submit a request despite the limitations outlined above, the request must:

1. Be issued by a valid government authority.
2. Be signed by a judge or magistrate.
3. Specifically identify the **Transaction Hash** or **Wallet Address** in question.
4. Cite the specific legal statute alleged to have been violated.

### 5.3. Standard Response: Statement of Incapacity

In response to valid orders to freeze funds or produce non-existent data, our legal counsel will file a formal **"Statement of Incapacity"** or **"Affidavit of No Records,"** attesting that:

1. Ownership has been renounced.
2. Compliance is technologically impossible.
3. No responsive records exist.

## ARTICLE VI: THE WEBSITE INTERFACE VS. THE PROTOCOL

LEAs must distinguish between the **Website Interface** (the GUI) and the **Protocol** (the Blockchain Logic).

### 6.1. Domain Seizure

If an LEA seizes the domain name `safwa.network` or shuts down the web hosting:

- **The Network Persists:** The Smart Contract on the Polygon Blockchain will continue to operate autonomously.
- **User Access:** Users will continue to interact with the Protocol via direct block explorer interaction or alternative, community-hosted front-ends.
- **Ineffectiveness:** Taking down the website does not stop the flow of funds, nor does it freeze assets.

## ARTICLE VII: NOTICE REGARDING AUTOMATED TAXATION

LEAs should be aware that the Protocol logic includes an automated function that directs a small percentage of transaction volume (0.1%) to a specific wallet address (`taxWallet`) to fund open-source development and maintenance.

- **Automated Nature:** This transfer is hard-coded and automatic. It does not imply active management or control of the network.
- **No Implication of Guilt:** The receipt of these automated tokens does not make the Deployers liable for the source of the funds (e.g., if a criminal uses the protocol).
