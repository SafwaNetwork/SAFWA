## ARTICLE V: VOLATILITY, TECHNICAL, AND ALGORITHMIC RISKS

While the Protocol is designed to be fully backed by reserves, specific technical risks affect your potential earnings:

### 5.1. Slippage

The "Price" displayed on the Interface is an **estimate** based on the current state of the blockchain.

- **Execution Price:** Due to the "Area Under the Curve" calculation mechanics, the effective price you pay (or receive) may be less favorable than the spot price, especially for large transactions. This difference ("Slippage") represents an immediate loss of value upon entry.

### 5.2. MEV and Front-Running

The Polygon Blockchain is a public ledger. Sophisticated actors and automated bots (MEV Bots) monitor the "Mempool" for pending transactions.

- **Sandwich Attacks:** A bot may detect your buy order, buy before you to raise the price, and sell immediately after you to profit from the difference. This extracts value from your trade and reduces your potential earnings.

### 5.3. Network Fees (Gas)

You are responsible for paying all transaction fees (Gas) required to interact with the Polygon Network.

- **Profit Erosion:** High network congestion may cause Gas fees to spike, potentially making small withdrawals or trades uneconomical. You must account for these costs when calculating potential returns.

### 5.4. The Sell Tax

You acknowledge that a **0.1% Protocol Tax** is automatically deducted from the gross value of every `sell` transaction.

- **Break-Even Requirement:** For you to exit the position without a loss, the token price must appreciate by a percentage sufficient to cover both the **0.1% Tax** and the **Gas Fees** for both the buy and sell transactions.
